🌱 FoodWise – AI Food Waste Reducer

FoodWise is an AI-powered Android application designed to help users reduce food waste, manage their food inventory efficiently, and make smarter decisions about food consumption. By combining intelligent recipe recommendations, expiry tracking, meal planning, and food management, FoodWise encourages sustainable living while saving money.

✨ Overview

Food waste is a major environmental and economic problem. Many households throw away food simply because they forget what they have stored or fail to use ingredients before they expire.

FoodWise addresses this problem by providing a smart digital solution that helps users track their food items, monitor freshness, discover recipes using available ingredients, and plan meals efficiently.

🚀 Features

- Food Inventory Management – Add, update, and manage food items with quantities, costs, storage locations, and dates.
- AI-Powered Recipe Generation – Generate personalized recipes using ingredients available in your inventory.
- Expiry Tracking – Monitor food items and identify those approaching their expiry dates.
- Smart Expiry Estimation – Estimate food freshness and storage duration based on food type, storage conditions, and ripeness.
- Meal Planning – Organize meals and plan food consumption in advance.
- Shopping List – Keep track of ingredients needed for future meals.
- Food Waste Analytics – Track food usage and understand your waste-reduction progress.
- Smart Notifications – Receive reminders about food items that need attention.
- Cloud Data Storage – Securely store and synchronize user data through Firebase.
- Food Storage Recommendations – Get useful tips to preserve food and extend its freshness.

📱 Application Modules

1. Dashboard

Provides an overview of food inventory, upcoming expiry items, food-saving statistics, and quick access to app features.

2. Inventory Management

Allows users to add and manage food items with details such as:

- Food name
- Quantity
- Unit
- Purchase or added date
- Expiry date
- Cost
- Storage location

3. AI Recipe Studio

Uses AI to suggest recipes based on available ingredients, helping users consume existing food instead of purchasing unnecessary ingredients.

4. Meal Planner

Helps users plan meals in advance and organize ingredient usage throughout the week.

5. Expiry Management

Tracks food freshness and highlights items that should be consumed soon, helping prevent unnecessary food disposal.

6. Impact & Analytics

Provides insights into food consumption, savings, and waste-reduction progress.
